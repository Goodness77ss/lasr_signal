## Reversible development choices

The first free starter roster uses three rifle roles. This is not approval of final class names, mint supply or recruitment prices. There are no automatic replacement purchases. A bank ends the first finite practice encounter. Reload resets this development run; no durable Journey recovery is promised before Step 09. The initial checkpoint award is ten simulated credits, a development tuning value with no wallet claim.

## Height traversal scope, 9 November 2026

J08: The owner explicitly selected full jumping and vaulting in the first prototype. Step 17 is required before proceeding to dependent class work. This includes physical vertical state, swept collision, ceiling and landing checks, shared player and enemy traversal constraints, matching camera and shots, durable recovery and an actual obstacle course. No height traversal is implemented merely by this decision.

## Acceptance journey

The complete target is roster selection, ongoing route travel, ambush response, checkpoint completion, a bank, verified participating soldier progress and a second wallet trade. Step 05 proves only the first finite local combat section. It does not prove generated regions, durability, ownership, chain progress or trading.

J12, 11 November 2026: The owner approved JOURNEY_SUPPLY_POLICY_PROPOSAL.md for disposable local development. Separate ERC1155 unopened packs contain three units each. Exact redemption burns packs for persistent nontransferable opened stock. Successful gameplay spends stock once; quit and death preserve unused stock. Health restores up to 35 HP, ammunition up to one magazine within reserve capacity, repair up to 25 armour, and grenades follow existing throw rules. Two confirmations and unique receipt identity precede credit. Orphaned confirmed redemption freezes affected spending for reconciliation. No public deployment or price is approved.


J13: Owner approved JOURNEY_FUNDED_POLICY_PROPOSAL.md for disposable local testing: no player deposit or stake, fifteen mock credits reserved before entry, ten checkpoint plus at most five eligible veteran loot, terminal bank, twenty minute deadline, forfeiture of unbanked new rewards on quit/death/deadline, unused pool backing released and independent timeout recovery. Owned assets and unused opened stock persist. No public deployment or real token economics approved.
