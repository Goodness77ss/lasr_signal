## Owner requested direction

An infinite destination based journey with checkpoints, escalating difficulty and distinct regions that affect gameplay. Begin with one squad. Expand it through collectible soldiers, and allow those soldiers to be listed for sale. Experience and furthest verified checkpoint should inform their history and asking price. Keep gun pickups. Some high ranking enemies carry recoverable WIF rewards. Add grenades, seven distinct soldier archetypes, more guns and supplies such as health packs. Retain NFT marketplace integration.

## Working assumptions requiring product review

The seven soldiers are archetypes, not a limit of seven NFTs in circulation. Players set listing prices; verified history informs buyers without guaranteeing resale value. Starter squad access does not require a wallet. Previously owned NFTs survive death or quitting; new unbanked rewards and loot can be lost. Tradeable supplies use consumable inventory rather than pretending each bandage is a permanent soldier NFT. These are recommendations, not recorded owner approvals.

The exact starter squad size, active squad cap, soldier loss rules, starting roster and reward schedule remain open. See the questions in the product brief before implementation begins.
